yt-trans — subtitles for YouTube videos with local models

A daemon with a certificate ([server] tls_cert in config.toml) listens only over https; without a certificate, only over http. Choosing manually is optional: if the selected protocol stays silent, the extension tries the other one itself. The check button does the same and remembers the one that works.
Cue source
For Serbian videos the author track is rare, but when it is there, it removes speech recognition entirely: both the minutes of GPU work and its errors. Turn it off if the track turned out to be not a transcript but a loose retelling, a translation into another language, or drifting from the speech — then our own recognition is more accurate. Switching restarts processing of the open video from scratch.
Second line
The second line is the Montenegrin translation of the same cue, for language learning. If the mode is disabled in the daemon config, the recognition text is shown. Cues that could not be translated always show the source text — it is highlighted in color and italics, and this setting will not duplicate it.
